## What that covers

Audio I hand to somebody: `apollo-v1.flac`, `flip.wav` as delivered into
callscape, anything attached to a release. Not the recipes, not the code.

## What you can do with those files

- Play them, copy them, post them, put them behind a podcast, loop them in a
  personal project.
- Cut, trim, layer and remix them.
- Credit: Daniel James Pritchett, and a link back here.

## What needs a conversation first

Anything commercial. A paid app, a client deliverable, an ad, a game you sell.
Ask me — the answer is usually yes.

## The obvious hole, named on purpose

The recipes are MIT and the renderer is deterministic to the byte, so you can
clone this repo, run `scpad render`, and get the same audio without touching my
files at all. That is intended, not an oversight. The code and the method are
free; the recordings I publish are the part I am keeping. If you want the sound
badly enough to build it yourself, you have earned it.
